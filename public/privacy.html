Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Shalom Marine Nets ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and place enquiries or orders, and how we use and protect it.

Information we collect

  • Contact & order details you provide: name, company, email, phone/ WhatsApp number, country, shipping address, and the products and quantities you request.
  • Verification data: a one-time passcode sent to your phone to confirm your identity when signing in.
  • Basic technical data: pages viewed and general usage, used only to keep the site working and improve it.

How we use it

  • To prepare quotes, process and ship orders, and provide customer support.
  • To send order confirmations and status updates.
  • To respond to your enquiries and improve our products and service.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example shipping, payment and messaging providers) and where required by law.

Data retention & security

We keep order and account information for as long as needed to provide our service and meet legal obligations, and we apply reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us using the details on our website and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the contact details on our website.
