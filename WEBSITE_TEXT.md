## FAQ (editable in Admin → SEO & Analytics)
- Eyebrow: **Help**
- Title: **Frequently Asked Questions**
- Q1: **What is the minimum order quantity?** — MOQ varies by product and is shown on each item. For bulk and export orders we offer flexible quantities — send us your requirement for a quote.
- Q2: **Do you ship worldwide?** — Yes. We export globally via sea freight (FCL/LCL), air freight and local courier. Lead times and Incoterms are confirmed per order.
- Q3: **Can nets be made to custom specifications?** — Absolutely. Mesh size, twine, depth, hangings and colours can all be made to order. Share your specs and we will manufacture to match.
- Q4: **How does payment work?** — Checkout places a quote-order with no online payment. We confirm pricing, stock and shipping, then send a payment request (Bank Transfer, Wire/TT, Letter of Credit and more). Prices shown include 18% GST.
- Q5: **Do you provide samples?** — Yes, samples are available on request for most products. Contact us with the item and specification you need.
